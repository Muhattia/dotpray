DOTPRAY / PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Dotpray for Android · com.dotpray.app

Dotpray is a prayer clock that runs on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no INTERNET permission. Prayer times are calculated on the device. Your location is used on the device to calculate those times and is stored locally; Dotpray does not send it to a Dotpray server.

Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, no Dotpray server. Location stays in the app's storage on your phone.

What Dotpray stores

Dotpray stores the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your saved location (place name, country, latitude, longitude, and time zone);
  • your settings (12/24-hour clock, theme choice, Asr school, and whether prayer alerts are enabled);
  • timestamps needed to refresh the widget and re-arm local alarms.

You can delete this data by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling Dotpray. There is no Dotpray account or server-side profile to delete.

Location

With your permission, Dotpray reads your approximate or precise location from Android to calculate prayer times and to ask the platform's geocoder for a place name.

  • The coordinates and place name are stored in the app's private preferences.
  • Dotpray does not upload them: the app declares no INTERNET permission and has no server.
  • If you do not grant location permission, or no fix is available, Dotpray uses Cairo as a neutral starting point.
  • The app re-checks the current location periodically while it is open, and when it resumes after the saved fix is more than 15 minutes old.

Platform geocoder. To display a place name, Dotpray calls Android's Geocoder. That is an operating-system service, not a Dotpray server. The device manufacturer or Google may process the request under its own privacy terms, depending on the device. Dotpray does not control that service.

Notifications and alarms

If you enable prayer alerts, Dotpray schedules local notifications for prayer times. This uses Android's notification and alarm APIs, including exact alarms when the operating system allows them. The alert content is generated locally. No notification data is sent to Dotpray.

Android backup

Android may back up app data if backup is enabled on your device. If so, your Dotpray preferences (including the saved location) may be included in your Google account's device backup. This is controlled by Android and your Google account settings, not by a Dotpray server. You can disable backup in Android settings.

What Dotpray does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.
  • No advertising or ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or rental of personal information.
  • No INTERNET permission.
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Sharing

Dotpray does not share personal information with third parties. The only non-Dotpray processing described above is performed by Android system services (the platform geocoder, notifications, alarms, and optional device backup) under the operating system's terms.

Security

Dotpray keeps its data in Android's app-private storage. Because the app has no INTERNET permission, it cannot transmit that data to a Dotpray server. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to minimise the data it handles.

Children

Dotpray is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no account or server-side profile.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will be published at the same URL. Material changes will also be reflected in the app or its store listing where appropriate.
