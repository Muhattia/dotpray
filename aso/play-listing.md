## Full description

`3461 / 4000 characters`

```text
Dotpray is a quiet prayer clock for Android, built around your home screen. It shows the next prayer, a calm countdown, and the full day's times — all calculated on your phone, without an account or an internet connection.

A PRAYER CLOCK THAT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY
No feed. No ads. No sign-up. Just the next prayer, its time, and a countdown that updates each minute. The monochrome dot-matrix design keeps the screen calm, with one red accent for the prayer that is next.

LIVE HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
• Wide 4×2 widget: the next prayer and countdown on the primary side, current time and dates opposite, and all five prayers across the bottom. Arabic mirrors the layout.
• Small 3×3 widget: the prayer name, time, countdown, and the elapsed-time rail.
• Minimal 2×2 widget: the same compact card without the rail.
• All three layouts keep resize handles and follow the app theme.
• Refreshes on the minute, at prayer boundaries, after reboot, and when the time zone changes.

AFTER THE ADHAN
• The prayer stays current for a short grace period: 15 minutes for Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, and Isha; 10 for Maghrib.
• During that time the app and all widgets show PRAYER NOW and a localized elapsed-time line, with the rail at 100%.
• When the window ends, the next prayer appears immediately, its countdown starts from zero, and the rail resets.

ENGLISH AND ARABIC
• Choose the app language in Settings. English is the default; العربية switches the whole interface to right-to-left.
• Prayer names, dates, place names, notifications and all widgets follow the selected language.
• Arabic uses the bundled Alexandria Regular and SemiBold faces, with natural joining and no dotted or letter-spaced treatment.
• Prayer times and the countdown stay in Doto in both languages. Nothing is downloaded at runtime.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
• Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha times for your current location.
• Next-prayer countdown with an elapsed-time progress rail in the app and every widget layout.
• Hijri date (Umm al-Qura) and Gregorian date.
• Qibla bearing from your coordinates.
• 12-hour or 24-hour clock.
• System, light, or dark theme — applied to the app and all widgets.
• Prayer alerts at each prayer time, exact when Android allows, localized to the app language.
• Works offline. No INTERNET permission.

ACCURATE, ON-DEVICE CALCULATION
Dotpray uses the NOAA solar position algorithm and the Egyptian General Authority of Survey method (Fajr 19.5°, Isha 17.5°). Asr follows the standard Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali school or the Hanafi shadow factor. Everything is computed locally; your coordinates are not uploaded.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts.
• No ads.
• No analytics or tracking SDKs.
• The app declares no INTERNET permission.
• Location is used on-device to calculate prayer times and show a place name. Before a fix is available, Cairo is used as a neutral starting point.
• Settings and location are stored in app storage on your phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Requires Android 8.0 or later.
• Alerts need notification permission; exact timing depends on Android's alarm settings and battery optimization.
• The calculation method is fixed to the Egyptian General Authority of Survey; there is no method picker.
• The interface is available in English and Arabic; Arabic is fully right-to-left.

Dotpray is made for people who want a calm, private prayer companion — not another app fighting for attention.
```

## Keywords and ASO strategy

### Primary cluster
`prayer times`, `offline prayer app`, `prayer widget`, `next prayer`

### Secondary cluster
`salah times`, `prayer clock`, `prayer alerts`, `qibla`, `Hijri date`,
`Muslim prayer app`, `Islamic prayer times`

### Long-tail intent
`offline prayer times widget`, `next prayer countdown`,
`prayer times without internet`, `ad-free prayer app`,
`private prayer app`, `prayer times no ads`,
`prayer times app for Android`

### Where each keyword lives
- **Title:** brand + offline + prayer times (the highest-weight field).
- **Short description:** prayer times + live widgets + alerts + Qibla + no ads/tracking.
- **First paragraph:** “prayer clock”, “next prayer”, “countdown”, “full day's times”, “without an account or an internet connection”.
- **Feature headings:** home-screen widgets, Fajr/Dhuhr/Asr/Maghrib/Isha, Qibla, Hijri, prayer alerts.
- **Screenshot text:** value propositions rather than a feature dump.

### Avoid
- **“Adhan”** — the app does not play the call to prayer audio. Use “prayer alerts”.
- **“Nothing OS”** — do not imply affiliation with Nothing. The design is described as monochrome dot-matrix.
- **“100% accurate everywhere”** — calculations are method-specific and high latitudes use a fallback.
- **“All schools / all methods”** — only the Egyptian General Authority method and two Asr schools are supported.
- **Untranslated Arabic screenshots** — the listing art is English. Arabic is a
  real interface option, but do not imply the screenshots themselves are Arabic.
- **Ramadan-only positioning** — Dotpray is a daily app; seasonal copy would narrow it.

### Positioning
> The calm, private prayer clock for people who want the next prayer on their
> home screen — not another noisy app.

The strongest conversion levers are:
1. the **countdown** (immediate utility);
2. the **three widget layouts** (home-screen habit);
3. **offline + no ads + no tracking** (trust and differentiation).
